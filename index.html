Who We Are

Dr Bites Dental Clinic is a trusted name in modern dental care, built around a simple idea: every patient should leave feeling confident, comfortable, and well-informed. From the first consultation to the final follow-up, we treat people the way we’d want our own family to be treated — with patience, honesty, and clinical excellence.

Located in Perumbakkam, Chennai, the clinic is led by experienced dental professionals who combine advanced training with genuine empathy. We invest in modern dental technology not because it’s impressive on a brochure, but because it leads to more accurate diagnosis, safer treatments, and faster recovery for the people who walk through our doors.

“Great dentistry isn’t just about clinical skill — it’s about earning trust, one patient at a time.”

Our Philosophy

Every treatment at Dr Bites begins with one question: what’s actually best for this patient? We don’t recommend procedures you don’t need. We don’t hide costs. We explain every option in language that makes sense, and we take the time to answer every question — even the ones you’ve been too nervous to ask.

That patient-first approach is what shapes everything else: the calm, hygienic environment, the careful sterilization protocols, the gentle pediatric care, and the way our team genuinely cares about how you’re feeling before and after every appointment.

What to Expect

Your first visit usually takes 30–45 minutes. We start with a thorough oral examination, discuss anything that’s been bothering you, and only then talk about treatment — in plain language, with no pressure. If diagnostics are needed, our in-clinic digital imaging means most results are ready the same day.

For follow-up treatments, we’ll always walk you through every step before we begin. Most procedures are virtually painless thanks to modern anaesthesia and gentle technique, and we’re happy to take breaks whenever you need one.
